ABOUT ME

I’m a passionate photographer who sees the world not as it is, but as light decides to reveal it. Every frame I chase is a quiet obsession — the golden second before sunset, the texture in a stranger’s gaze, the geometry hiding in everyday streets.

From misty mountain ridgelines to the blue glow of an empty city at 3 AM, I photograph whatever makes my pulse quicken. Photography isn’t just what I do — it’s how I make sense of the world.
